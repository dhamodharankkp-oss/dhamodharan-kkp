# Research Overview

### Open Research in Additive Manufacturing for Educational Toy Kits

by **Dhamodharan KKP**  
*Makerspace Educator, Design Engineer & Independent Researcher*

---

#### 🎯 Vision

To explore and openly share sustainable, affordable, and pedagogically rich 3D printed educational toys and learning manipulatives that empower students and teachers to engage in hands-on STEM learning aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals (**SDG 4: Quality Education**, **SDG 9: Industry & Innovation**, and **SDG 12: Responsible Consumption**).

---

#### 📖 About This Research

This project documents the open research journey in:
* **Designing 3D Printed Educational Kits:** Creating tactile toys and geometric models that ground abstract mathematical and scientific principles.
* **Pedagogical Integration:** Aligning tools with constructivist learning theories, the **5E Instructional Model**, and the **4 Ps of Creative Learning** (MIT Media Lab Lifelong Kindergarten).
* **Design for Additive Manufacturing (DfAM):** Optimizing print orientation, overhangs, print-in-place tolerances, and material properties.
* **Sustainable Materials:** Experimenting with biodegradable, recycled PLA and eco-friendly filaments for low-cost, decentralized school fabrication.
* **Open Educational Resources (OER):** Sharing printable files, parametric code, and lesson plans openly.

#### 🔬 Active Research Focus Areas

| Theme | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Additive Manufacturing for Education** | Applying desktop 3D printing for rapid, accessible, decentralized toy and kit fabrication. |
| **Parametric Model Generation with AI** | Combining **OpenSCAD** code and **Agentic AI workflows** with **Design-Based Research (DBR)** to generate customized math manipulatives. |
| **Sustainable Filaments** | Testing recycled PLA, biodegradable polyesters, and local material alternatives. |
| **Design for Additive Manufacturing (DfAM)** | Engineering print-in-place joints, snap fits, and compliant mechanisms without external fasteners. |
| **STEAM & Maker Pedagogy** | Integrating physical making into formal and informal science and engineering learning environments. |
| **Open Source Hardware (OSH)** | Releasing reproducible models under open licenses for community remixing. |

#### 🚀 Key Projects Under This Initiative

1. [**Parametric Math Manipulatives Baseline Generator**](projects/toy-designs-and-products/parametric-math-manipulatives.md)  
   *Translating empirical literature on spatial reasoning and early math interventions into dynamic OpenSCAD parametric code via Agentic AI workflows.*
2. [**MorphPlay: Circle to Sphere**](projects/toy-designs-and-products/morphplay-circle-to-sphere.md)  
   *Kinetic topological toy exploring continuous dimensional transitions from flat discs into three-dimensional spherical geometries.*
3. [**Turtle Art – Mathematical Patterns**](projects/tinkering/turtle-art/turtle-art-mathematical-patterns.md)  
   *Transforming algorithmic block coding into vector SVG curves and physical 3D-printed geometric reliefs.*
4. [**OctoStudio & MIT Media Lab LCL Reflections**](projects/tinkering/creative-coding/octostudio-lcl.md)  
   *Mobile creative coding, physical computing, sensor integration, and makerspace pedagogy at Aarvam Learning Lab.*

#### 🎯 Research Objectives

1. Develop a comprehensive library of open-source printable learning kits for K–12 and foundational education.
2. Evaluate educational impact through classroom trials in rural and community learning centers.
3. Promote sustainability through modularity, print minimization, and recycled polymer utilization.
4. Foster cross-disciplinary collaboration among educators, makers, and students.
5. Contribute to global Open Science, citizen research, and UN Sustainable Development Goals.
