# Research Overview

### Open Research in Additive Manufacturing for Educational Toy Kits

by **Dhamodharan KKP**  
*Independent Researcher & Design Contributor*

---

## Goal

To design and openly share affordable, 3D-printable educational toys and math manipulatives that support hands-on STEM learning, aligned with **SDG 4 (Quality Education)**, **SDG 9 (Industry & Innovation)**, and **SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption)**.

---

## What This Research Covers

* **3D Printed Educational Kits:** Tactile toys and geometric models that make abstract math and science concepts concrete.
* **Pedagogical Alignment:** Grounding physical models in constructivist learning, the **5E Instructional Model**, and the **4 Ps of Creative Learning** (Projects, Passion, Peers, Play).
* **Design for Additive Manufacturing (DfAM):** Tuning print orientation, overhang angles, print-in-place tolerances, and shell thickness.
* **Sustainable Materials:** Testing biodegradable and recycled PLA filaments for low-cost, local fabrication.
* **Open Educational Resources (OER):** Sharing STL/3MF files, OpenSCAD scripts, and notes under open licenses.

---

## Focus Areas

| Theme | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Additive Manufacturing for Education** | Using desktop 3D printing for local, low-cost fabrication of learning kits. |
| **Parametric Modeling with AI** | Combining **OpenSCAD** scripts and AI-assisted workflows with **Design-Based Research (DBR)** to build customizable math manipulatives. |
| **Sustainable Filaments** | Testing recycled PLA and biodegradable polymers. |
| **DfAM Engineering** | Designing print-in-place joints, snap fits, and compliant mechanisms without external fasteners. |
| **Maker Pedagogy** | Connecting physical making with computational thinking and open inquiry. |
| **Open Source Hardware** | Releasing reproducible models and code for anyone to print and remix. |

4. [**OctoStudio & MIT Media Lab LCL Reflections**](projects/tinkering/creative-coding/octostudio-lcl.md) — Notes on mobile creative coding, sensor projects, and constructionist learning.

---

## Objectives

1. Build an open library of printable learning kits for K–12 and foundational math/science.
2. Study classroom durability and ergonomics through Design-Based Research cycles.
3. Reduce material waste through modular design and recycled filaments.
4. Share reproducible CAD files and documentation for educators and makerspaces.
