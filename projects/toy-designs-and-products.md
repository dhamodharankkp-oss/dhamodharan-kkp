# Toy Designs, Products & EdTech Manipulatives

Physical kinetic toys, math manipulatives, and parametric 3D-printed models built using Design-Based Research (DBR) and Design for Additive Manufacturing (DfAM).

---

### Documented Projects

* [**MorphPlay – Circle to Sphere**](toy-designs-and-products/morphplay-circle-to-sphere.md)  
  Kinetic toy demonstrating the transition from a flat circular disc into a 3D sphere.

* [**Numicon Math Manipulatives (Shapes 1–10)**](toy-designs-and-products/numicon-math-shapes.md)  
  DBR redesign of arithmetic tiles with shell-dominant slicing optimization for classroom durability.

* [**Tamil Mensuration 3D Solids (அளவியல்)**](toy-designs-and-products/tamil-mensuration-openscad.md)  
  OpenSCAD parametric 3D-printed models for Tamil Nadu Samacheer Kalvi Class 10 Math Chapter 7 (உருளை, கூம்பு, கோளம், இடைக்கண்டம்).

* [**Tactile Patterned Cone Surface**](toy-designs-and-products/cone-surface-tactile-toy.md)  
  Fusion 360 compound solid with a patterned dimple lattice on the conical surface and a rounded spherical tip.

* [**Parametric Math Manipulative Baseline Generator (AI + OpenSCAD)**](toy-designs-and-products/parametric-math-manipulatives.md)  
  Parametric OpenSCAD generator linking spatial reasoning research with 3D-printable math manipulatives.
